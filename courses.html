Three boards, taught by specialists.

Here's what we cover for each board and how we coach it. Every student starts with a free diagnostic before any plan begins.

Grades 6 to 12

IB (MYP & Diploma)

  • MYP coaching (grades 6 to 10) aligned to the MYP criteria
  • IB Diploma HL & SL subject coaching (grades 11 to 12)
  • Internal Assessment (IA) and extended-essay support
  • Mock exams marked to the IB grade descriptors
  • A smooth bridge from MYP into the Diploma

Grades 9 to 10

Cambridge IGCSE

  • Core & Extended coaching across Maths, Sciences, English and more
  • Taught to the Cambridge assessment objectives, not just the textbook
  • Past-paper practice marked to the Cambridge scheme

  • Sets up a clean transition into A Levels or the IB Diploma

ICSE
