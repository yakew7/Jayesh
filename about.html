About JLC

A Legacy of Excellence.
A Future of Possibilities.

Founded by Prof. Jayesh Shethia, JLC is built on a simple belief: that every student has the potential to achieve beyond limits with the right guidance, discipline and support.

A seasoned educator with a passion for making learning an adventure, Prof. Jayesh Shethia has, for over 24 years, inspired over 25,000 students across ICSE, IB, IGCSE, CBSE & Maharashtra State Board. A master of Mathematics, he has authored various titles for Navneet Publications and enriched online learning for students in the USA, UAE and select European nations.
