Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the admission process?

It is a simple five-step journey: enquire, counselling, a short assessment, a programme recommendation and enrollment. Once enrolled, we begin with an orientation to welcome your child to JLC.

Is the assessment difficult?

Not at all. The assessment is a friendly evaluation to understand your child's current level and learning needs so we can place them in the right batch. It's not a pass-or-fail test.

Can I change batch later?

Yes. If a different batch or timing suits your child better, our team will help you switch subject to seat availability in the preferred batch.

Are scholarships available?

We offer merit-based support for deserving students. Speak to our admission team during counselling to learn about the current scholarship options.

What documents are required?

Typically the previous year's report card, a copy of your child's school ID and basic contact details. Our team will share the exact checklist during enrollment.

Do you offer sibling discounts?

Yes, families enrolling more than one child are eligible for a sibling benefit. Please ask our admission team for the current details.
