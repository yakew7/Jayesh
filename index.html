Our approach

How we mark our own work.

We hold ourselves to the same standard we hold a student's exam script to. Four rules we don't break.

1

Diagnose before teaching

No student is booked into a plan until a diagnostic session shows exactly where the gap is.

2

Specialists, not generalists

A tutor is matched to the student's board and subject, not handed whatever is free.

3

Practice under real conditions

Regular assessments and mock exams run to real board timing and marking, not soft practice.

4

Direct parent reporting

You hear from us after every assessment: where the grade is, and what we're doing next.
