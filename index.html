About the Founder

Meet Professor Jayesh

With 35 years of teaching excellence, Professor Jayesh has mentored generations of students across ICSE, IGCSE, IB and Olympiads. His clarity of concept, patience and unwavering commitment have turned learning into a joyful, life-changing journey for every child who walks through the doors of JLC.

He founded Jayesh Learning Centre on a simple belief — that every student can excel when taught with care, structure and genuine passion. That philosophy continues to shape everything we do.
