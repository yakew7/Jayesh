Frequently Asked Questions

What classes and courses do you offer?

We offer coaching for ICSE and IGCSE (Grades 6–10), the IB programme (MYP 1–5 and IBDP), and Olympiad preparation for Grades 3–10.

How can I enrol my child at JLC?

Simply book a free counselling session, or call us at +91 85918 77127. Our team will guide you through the assessment and enrolment process.

What are the timings of classes?

Our centre is open Monday to Saturday, 11:30 AM to 7:00 PM. Batch timings are scheduled to suit school hours — please contact us for the current schedule.

Do you provide online classes?

Yes. We offer both in-centre and online learning options so students can continue their preparation from anywhere.
