FAQs

Answered before you ask.

The questions parents ask most. Still unsure about something? Message us on WhatsApp.

How does the free demo / diagnostic class work, and is it really free?

Yes, genuinely free. Before any student is booked into a plan, we run a diagnostic session to assess current standing subject by subject and set an honest target grade. There's no obligation to enrol afterwards, you decide once you've seen the plan.

Is coaching one-to-one, small batch, or both, and how are batches sized?

Both. We offer one-to-one coaching and small batches, chosen to suit the student and subject. Batches are deliberately kept small so every student gets individual attention. [PLACEHOLDER: confirm exact batch-size limits with the client.]

How is a tutor matched to my child's board and subject?

We match a subject specialist to the student's specific board and subject, not whoever is free. A tutor coaching IB Diploma HL Physics knows that syllabus and its marking; the same care applies across IGCSE, A Level, ICSE and MYP.

What do fees look like, and how are they paid?

Fees depend on the programme, subjects and whether coaching is one-to-one or small batch. They're agreed directly once a plan is set, typically monthly, per term or per package. [PLACEHOLDER: confirm fee structure and accepted payment modes with the client.]

What if a class needs to be rescheduled or is missed?

Let us know in advance and we'll do our best to arrange a make-up class. Full details are in our Terms & Conditions. [PLACEHOLDER: confirm notice period and make-up policy.]

How often do parents get progress updates?

After every assessment. You'll hear where the grade is now, and exactly what we're doing next to move it, not just a note at the end of term.

Do you teach online, in person, or both?

We coach at our centre in Mulund West and can arrange online sessions where it suits the student. [PLACEHOLDER: confirm online availability with the client.]

What is the cancellation or refund policy if we stop mid-package?
