Our approach

How we mark our own work.

We hold our coaching to the same standard we hold a student's exam script to. Here's exactly how a term runs, and the four rules we don't break.

1

Diagnose before teaching

No student is booked into a plan until a diagnostic session shows exactly where the gap is. We don't teach in the dark.

2

Specialists, not generalists

A tutor is matched to the student's board and subject, a specialist who knows that syllabus and its marking, not whoever happens to be free.

3

Practice under real conditions

Assessments and mock exams run to real board timing and marking schemes. The grade on the day should be the grade we've already seen.

4

Direct parent reporting

You hear from us after every assessment, where the grade is now, and precisely what we're doing next to move it.

How a term runs

From first session to board day.

Step 01

Diagnostic session

A free diagnostic reads the student's current standing subject by subject, and sets an honest target grade. Nobody enrols before this.

Step 02

Individual study plan

We build a plan around the specific gap, which topics, in which order, at what pace, and match a subject specialist to teach it.

Step 03

Regular assessments

Short, frequent assessments keep the plan honest and catch slippage early, before it costs marks on the board paper.

Step 04

Mock exams, real conditions

Full mocks run to real timing and are marked to the board's own scheme, so exam-day holds no surprises.

Step 05

Parent reporting

After every assessment you get a direct report: current grade, target grade, and the next move. No vague reassurance.

Step 06

Adjust & repeat

The plan flexes to the data. As the gap closes we raise the target, the goal is always the grade above the last one.
